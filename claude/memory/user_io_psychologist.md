User is an Industrial-Organizational (I-O) psychologist. They think in terms of I-O constructs and measurement theory (formative vs reflective constructs, withdrawal literature, career mobility, person-job fit). They care about construct validity when operationalizing behavioral indicators. They prefer building analyses incrementally (descriptive -> correlational -> predictive -> causal) rather than jumping to grand hypotheses. They are careful about causal claims and aware of limitations of single-method designs.
